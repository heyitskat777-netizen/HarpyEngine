## Branching scene

Anchor sees a report of an amber reflection. She pencils a question mark on her
map and asks the pilot to verify it from the public platform. Both branches
keep the concern open through a quiet turn.

- The pilot confirms a service mirror. Anchor says, “Copy your identification.
  I mark the reflection as reported.” The new description stays attributed to
  the pilot.
- The pilot declines to move from her position. Anchor says, “Understood. I can
  call Venn if you want another option.” The approved **Introduce the support**
  Play creates Venn through the native workflow. The pilot retains the choice
  of whether to use that option.

An actual heard report from the original speaker qualifies the reviewed
follow-up. The confirmation also exercises the tactical-update path and checks
that retrying the input cannot produce a second reply. The original Handler
previews and confirms delivery to the GM-approved recipients.
The recipient sees the dialogue and action in scene order, with the actors and
controllers fixed on the receipt. Only listeners and the Handler gain the
attributed memory. The bystander sees neither contribution. After delivery
addresses the concern, ordinary chatter returns to WAIT. Each branch carried
its actual memory into a later scene. Concern tracking itself is scoped to the
current scene. It does not assume a response or generate one on a timer.
