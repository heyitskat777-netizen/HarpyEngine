# Actual participation in ordinary NPC use

Source: Easy Target, rulebook PDF41 / printed39. The cohort option exempts both
participants from its listed consequences. This additive v10 extension implements
that ordinary route, rather than forcing a non-triggered Play or fabricating a
Tag modifier or dice receipt.

After all original reactions settle and the original Handler validates non-Play
use, each original cohort uses their private native control to record actual
participation while use is ongoing. Immutable UseCohort facts bind original
reaction, source, controller, answer, scene and available co-present body.
Nomination alone grants no completed performance. The original NPC controller
records completion only after all selected cohorts have performed. Both original
obligations then complete with the printed exemption; no optional Bond choice is
created. No roll, injury, payment, dialogue, intimacy consent or unrelated effect
is inferred. Existing non-cohort use and rolled-child workflows stay distinct.

History proof verifies original owned choice and body evidence, and private
service readback exposes the participant's own record. The actual original NPC
still owns completion. A completed use cannot acquire retroactive participation.
The delegated interpretation remains marked for printed-rule clarity review.
Other performer body kinds and performances triggering another real source remain
outside this bounded adapter. This does not establish whole-P1 completion.
