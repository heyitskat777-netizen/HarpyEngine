## Personal cues and authored actions

The same line about still hearing an alarm led to four verified paths.

- **Heard.** The Handler offered a cup of tea and company to the original Pilot.
  A second Pilot could not receive that personal delivery. A replay or repetition
  of the cue created no duplicate contribution.
- **Private.** The review rejected the unheard cue. The Handler provider was
  never invoked.
- **Peer responds first.** A reviewed later reply from a different Pilot,
  heard by both the original speaker and Handler, kept the route silent.
- **Peer responds before delivery.** The same evidence blocked an already
  approved and undelivered Handler line.

No new scheduler or reply quota is involved. The existing review path supplies
the semantic judgment about whether the later reply attended to the cue. The
engine checks chronology, the current revision, actual speech and the listeners.

The follow-up trial had the Handler take her own checklist to the control desk
and leave an open question. A second Pilot who heard that contribution later
reported an actual console assignment. The Handler stayed within that report
and told the original Pilot. The follow-up closed on delivery. The fixture also
verified that a follow-up sent solely to the reporter was rejected.
