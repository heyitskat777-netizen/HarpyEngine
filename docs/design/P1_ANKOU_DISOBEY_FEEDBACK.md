original receiver chooses Disobey and opens that exact native response wizard,
marking her last Identity. Mandatory Ankou Feedback holds the actual Order child
without rolling or consuming the next-refusal bonus. Original person-owned
Feedback effects resolve before the original refusal and its printed outcomes.

receiver-authored choice and pending child request are proved, not synthesized.

events/request and the originally declared real Strain bonus list. Current
superior authority and the pending original request are revalidated before the
last mark and again before the actual refusal roll.

validation now precedes the Feedback hold while consumption follows it. The
source's real tear/Flirt reprisal cannot consume the next Disobey bonus. A refused
purpose becomes actual Helpless, closes the original unrolled source and its
Receive Orders parent, and does not claim successful disobedience, draw dice or
consume the bonus. Successful Feedback resumes the same source; its actual
refusal retains witnessed Status loss, owned high immediate-action confirmation,
low inability to disobey, and Handler discovery/Punishment consequences. Original
receipt and parent completion survive reread/replay.
