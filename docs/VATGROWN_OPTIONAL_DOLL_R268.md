neither benefit. Taking the optional benefit package still requires exactly two;
this does not reinterpret “pick 2” as “up to 2.” Decline cannot be mixed with a
benefit or a question. High/low existing one-benefit admission and their
object/attention/Punishment effects are unchanged.

The original roll, Heat cost, source, selected response event and completion
are retained. Decline records no fabricated Effect/Information/Departure.
Historical proof reconstructs the empty benefits from the exact original
owned decline response; an absent response is not a completed decline.
Normal two-benefit historical choices remain valid. No schema/registry
approval, player consent, or production release gate was relaxed.
The all-or-none reading is a delegated interpretation, not uniquely established
by the wording: see CAMPAIGN_RULINGS.md and registry ruling
`vatgrown-doll-mid-optional-benefits`. Printed clarity review is pending; this
registry change changes the campaign digest and requires matching legitimate
approval before normal production admission. Low can-pick wording is not
independently interpreted by this patch.
