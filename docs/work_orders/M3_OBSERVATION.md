# M3E — Void Observation and separately played roles

Status: IN_PROGRESS / RULES_SUBSET. Sources: book PDF 28, 123 and 192;
printed pages 26, 121 and 190. Existing explicit campaign decisions apply.

Implemented offline: the current Handler records its actual suspicion before the
Girl reserves its Off-Duty Play. Existing Observation debts are bound to that
actual period. Observation replaces the ordinary roll, lowers actual Status in the
locked roll transaction, and uses the number of Void Assets across both families,
including damaged Assets. No Heat is spent. The shared actual dice, Tag/Bond
adjustments, saved decisions, pause, abort/resume and chat restoration protocol
are retained. Cancelling an unrolled reservation preserves the Observation order.

The player selects one consequence. The Handler selects zero, one or two for the
actual final low/mid/high total. Hiding suspicion can be reviewed after the roll,
before the first human submits selections. Lists and nested choices remain owned
and revision-bound. Surgery selects an actual current Void Asset of the right
family. Reprogramming is a separate affected-player request using the real reset,
protected slots and approved fallback. Populace Tags have an actual human giver;
Harpy Candidate keeps its fixed label. Become Dog changes the current rank order.
Medical examination/hospital outcomes record actual Handler-authored consequences.
Moderate Punishment requires an actual Moderate discipline effect, not a scene or
a substituted Minor type. Observation's low result uses its own printed outcomes;
the ordinary low-result on-screen discipline restriction is not copied onto it.

Actual NPC assignment/arrival uses a living, available, appropriate role. A
separately controlled NPC receives its own saved question. Declining returns the
proposal without an appearance; accepting applies it once. Director introductions
use an invited human different from the Handler under the recorded campaign
default. The printed separate-player requirement is specific to High Budget 1/1;
generic NPC control is broader. Invitations preserve the
proposed private NPC and require actual acceptance before introduction. Accepting
a role, accepting an appearance and confirming a played scene are separate actions.
Non-Pilot members can own these roles without fabricated Pilot sheets. Individual
requests grant only their scoped access; actual scene invitations permit their
own scene confirmation. Required NPC scene controllers cannot be substituted.

After selections, the Girl chooses a selected consequence or printed harsh scene;
each actual participant confirms their own contribution. Recovery waits for real
effects and scene completion. Completing that Observation fulfills only its bound
debts. Pending Frame bonuses, other effects and the Handler review remain required.
Native private controls and the inbox expose orders, suspicion review, own choices,
actual details, separate roles and scene invitations.

Still required: complete Girl Play ports for Director presence and price rules; automatic
world-trigger/reprimand and future-period ordering; all battle/last-Asset,
playbook, Defeat, shortage and economy interactions; explicit recovery for a changed
NPC controller. A Director arrival creates blocking `director_presence` work.
The Handler cannot keep using ordinary Handler rule authority while its presence
review is unresolved or its actual Girl-rules context is active. There is no generic acknowledgement/waiver for it.
This is an honest coverage gap, not a new campaign ruling or full M3 release.

Acceptance: all final tier counts, Void family/damage count, pre-roll commitment,
no ordinary substitute, exact owned selections, scoped hidden choices, real nested
Identity/Tag/Punishment effects, distinct choices, interrupted/resumed rolls,
unrolled cancellation, separate Director and non-Pilot role responses, independent

and real synthetic backup/restore. Independent and human tests remain pending.
