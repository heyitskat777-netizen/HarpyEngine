# M3D/E — Permissions and actual purchased benefits

Status: IN_PROGRESS / RULES_SUBSET. Source: book PDF 16 / printed p.14;
Asset limits PDF 15 / printed p.13; the official base Frame Asset lists.
Keep the owner-approved price of one Permission for the unpriced standard
Requisitions. Extra shortage repairs retain the separate actual Handler quotes.

Implement an immutable, character/Frame-bound purchase ledger in the existing
canonical state. The player explicitly chooses and confirms each purchase. Record
the actual source event, paid cost and acquired benefit. Transactions must atomically
save resources, the actual benefit, immutable history and its source. Preserve
existing state; views, stale forms, replay, concurrency and delivery cannot charge
again or grant another Asset. Retain private descriptions outside public audit/posts.

First implement Asset Installment: one printed Asset from any official base Frame,
or an owner-authored custom Asset as the source permits. New Assets are ordinary
and undamaged; purchases do not grant a Frame playbook, intrinsic rules, Void or
another character's control. At five Frame Assets explicitly replace one actual
non-Void Frame Asset. Validate actual character, current Frame, funds, pause,
pending Plays/immediate work and restored chat. Require the owner to describe
the actual installation in the Conversation; purchasing never moves a body,
creates custody, repairs a different Asset or waives a prior consequence.

Then implement the printed delayed benefits: Creature Comforts and Visiting Home
qualify on an actual future 8+ Go Off-Duty roll, after real post-roll modifiers;
they require an actual owned scene, not automatic grants. Privacy requires the
chosen Girl's actual participation and an uninterrupted private scene. Silence is
not an answer or attendance. Link each owed benefit and participating character
to its original purchase/source. Resolve optional opportunities only from the
player's actual decision. Recovery/end must respect required pending choices.

Ability Unlock, Frame Grafting/Swap, Retirement, revocation and their playbook,
career/control consequences remain separate required work. Do not label a purchase
ledger or one implemented Requisition as complete economy coverage. No live
migration, gameplay deployment, environment publication, AI or M4 is authorized.

Acceptance includes printed/custom selections, caps and protected Void Assets,
wrong family/price/owner, insufficient funds, stale sheet/Frame/body/profile,
policy/pause, concurrent purchase/replay, injected rollback, restart/rebuild,
private projections and owned paginated native controls. Delayed benefits also
need actual qualifying receipts, low rolls, known-roll recovery, per-human scene
attestation, declined opportunities and preservation across period boundaries.
