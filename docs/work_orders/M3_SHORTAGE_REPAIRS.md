# M3D/E/F — Low Budget repair shortage and exact paid repairs

Status: IN_PROGRESS / RULES_SUBSET. No live deployment or human acceptance.
Sources: book PDF 150 / printed p.148 (Low Budget 4/2), PDF 15–16 / printed
pp.13–14 (Assets and Permissions), and the owner's per-repair Handler-price ruling.
The delegated next-actual-dispatch interpretation is recorded centrally with its
alternative and clarity-review/change-impact notes.

The saved Strain event arms one actual restriction. Since this lifecycle begins
Off-Duty only after all missions finish, the event binds to the next real accepted
mission dispatch, its actual end and the following recovery period. It does not
retroactively restrict the already-ended mission or current recovery. Keep every
source work, dispatch event, end event and period ID. Repeated scopes retain the
same one-Asset quota per family rather than granting additional free repairs.

The player explicitly selects up to the actual available quota: one damaged Girl
Asset and one damaged Frame Asset. Their own recovery commits once, and remaining
damage persists. Native recovery selectors use actual owned damaged Asset IDs;
normal unrestricted recovery remains separate. A shortage's actual completed
period closes its effect; a timer or unavailable person never advances it.

After actual free recovery, the current Handler may offer one exact Permission
price per damaged Asset. Quotes record real character and Frame incarnations,
sheet/quote revisions, actual shortage/period and source event. A changed price,
sheet, Frame, character, Handler or closed period makes the old purchase fail.
Neither an offer, reopening a view nor a disconnected user buys anything. The
owner separately accepts or declines. Actual repair, charge, response and private
history commit atomically, with idempotent replay and no dice.

`/mission repairs` provides private offer, repricing and purchase controls. Pending
owner offers also appear in the guided requests inbox. The Handler sees only the
Asset labels needed to quote; unrelated users do not receive a quote or its price.
Paused safety permits an OOC decline, while spending/repairing stays blocked.
Existing Play/chat restoration, release approval and normal purchase affordability
checks remain enforced. Paid optional offers do not falsely become compulsory work
or prevent actual period completion.

The feature covers actual Girl/Frame Assets, not Casualty body-part rules or all
Requisitions. Remaining playbook/passive, economy, career and world interactions
retain their gates. Acceptance must cover a real source-event/mission/recovery
sequence, exact family quota, replay/concurrency, rollback, changed identities and
Frames, pause/policy revocation, stale/revised prices, actual period close,
unrestored chat and native player confirmation. Independent source review and
human multiplayer testing remain separate milestones of acceptance.
