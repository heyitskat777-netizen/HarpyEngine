## Character creation

Create pilot uses HarpyEngine's Girl and Frame catalog. The dossier shows the selected playbooks' personal fields and native review requirements. Enrollment assignments and the Inbox expose shared Court, Mentor, Pair Asset, standard, and investor-desire choices to the accounts that own them.

Restart character is on the active sheet owner's Pilot Profile. The owner explicitly approves and types the exact callsign. HarpyEngine archives the original incarnation, then enrollment starts a new character. The Handler assigns the replacement's cadre position.

It also checks reopening, cross-owner rejection, and replay of the debrief and Off-Duty transitions. `node verify-mission.cjs --mission-desk-check` exercises the Missions folder through Off-Duty preparation and verifies the saved debrief and period after restarting the isolated lab.

- Two-account confrontation tests check that applying effects, selecting private resources, answering a pin, and applying assigned discipline use the proper signed-in account. The original roll and authorized result survive a restart.
