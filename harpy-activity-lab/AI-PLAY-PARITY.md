- A prepared AI dossier belongs to its controller's own sheet. The generic NPC seat exposes dialogue and reviewed intent. The source and engine limitations section records the independent initiative boundary and how already assigned alternate-cast responses stay with their controllers.

- Registry source/review state and the isolated campaign’s digest-bound policy remain authoritative. An individually disabled native branch needs an exact gate, rather than enabling a whole book.
